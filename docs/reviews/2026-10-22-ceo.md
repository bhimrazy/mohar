## 2. Right things, right order, for a Nepali host?

The switch path is now real for a small host on CSV: install, NPR and VAT, import clients, link cPanel accounts,
`.np` guidance, eSewa. The order is no longer the problem; the mix is.

- **Cut or cap: KYC and Redactor polish.** KYC is off by default and needed only for `.np` hosts that choose it.
  Re-encoding (#286), browser resize (#312, issue #313 already filed on its interaction), unlock races (#308), history
  clearing (#249, #304) and the backfill (#287) are good work at the wrong time. Finish #287 and #308 (they protect
  stored data), park #312, #313, #298, #303, #309, #311 until a host turns KYC on.
- **Pull forward: Nepal wallet simulators (#208, #177).** The owner chose simulators as the proof for payments, but
  only Stripe and WHM have one. eSewa and Khalti, the two methods every Nepali customer uses, have neither a simulator
  nor (Khalti) a live run. Under the 10-22 decision this is the only route to evidence; it is labelled "low".
- **Pull forward: a VAT sales register and credit notes (roadmap 3.8, 3.9).** A VAT-registered host files a monthly
  return from a sales book and corrects issued invoices with credit notes. Without both, month-end goes back to
  spreadsheets in week four. A tax-liability / sales-register CSV is a small slice of Reports.
- **Next integration: ResellerClub.** The registrar contracts merged today (#262–#265) were the prerequisite. `.com`
  is the other half of every Nepali host's domain sales; its demo reseller account is free, which fits the
  no-paid-accounts decision.
- **Then connectIPS against a simulator** from the public spec (#128), so it is ready when the owner enrols.
- **Keep deferred:** social sign-in, affiliates, AI, WhatsApp/SMS, medium and large load profiles, cPanel PR 2.

## 3. Risks to the business

Go-live blockers for paying Nepali customers:

- **Nepal tax compliance, unchanged since 10-19.** Whether IRD electronic billing and CBMS reporting apply to a
  VAT-registered host billing by software, and fiscal-year numbering, still wait for an accountant. If CBMS applies,
  a VAT host cannot issue tax invoices from the product. BS dates (#293) cover the date question only.
- **Payments have no proof for the go-live method mix.** eSewa: UAT partly passed on 10-20, happy path pending.
  Khalti: no live run, no simulator, no hosted resume (#251). connectIPS, Fonepay, cards: spec only. The 10-22
  decision defers live accounts, so the date of the first real NPR payment is set by the owner, not the build.
- **Server cost message is contradictory.** `sizing.md` measures 4 GB as enough for 50k clients; `production.md`
  says 8 GB is the minimum for the blue-green compose stack. A host reading the install page will price an 8 GB VPS.
- **Backups.** Documented (`production.md`, docs site) but no restore has ever been run, on a runner or anywhere.
- **Data.** KYC holds citizenship scans, encrypted, 90-day retention; images stored before #286 keep EXIF until
  #287 lands. Key rotation now exists (#253). The repository is public; licence all rights reserved.
- **Capacity.** 100 open issues, rising by about 10 a day, mostly review follow-ups without an owner. Work moved to
  one Mac after the codespace limit; Fable's limit resets 10-25.

Owner dependencies, by lead time: connectIPS enrolment (weeks; now deferred by decision), accountant on CBMS and
fiscal-year numbering, eSewa and Khalti merchant accounts, licence and NPR price, support model, trademark search in
Nepal, branch protection, a design-partner host in Kathmandu.

## 4. A Nepali host's first week (400 clients, WHMCS, cPanel reseller, .com.np and .com, VAT registered)

- **Day 1, install.** 20-second Docker install with HTTPS, Nepal defaults (NPR, VAT 13%, Kathmandu clock), PHP
  floor check. The docs ask for 8 GB; the host has 4 GB and either overpays or guesses. Mail is `.env` only, no test send.
- **Day 2, company and money.** Seller PAN, VAT, lakh grouping, optional BS date: better than WHMCS out of the box.
  The accountant asks about CBMS and the sales book: no answer, no report.
- **Day 3, clients.** WHMCS client export through the CSV importer: map, check, review duplicates, apply, report,
  undo window. Works. Unpaid invoices and domains do not come across; staff re-raise open invoices by hand and type
  every domain. Invitations go out at 100/hour, four hours for 400.
- **Day 3, servers.** cPanel connect and account import link services well; package names typed by hand.
- **Day 4, payments.** First real eSewa payment is the first ever on a live merchant. Khalti untested. A customer who
  asks for connectIPS or card in NPR has no option.
- **Day 5, domains.** `.np` guidance with nameservers works. Every `.com` order is manual: no registrar driver.
- **Week end.** No reports, no credit notes, no Nepali language.

What keeps them: invoice and ledger correctness, the import with undo, cPanel linking, branded email, security.
What makes them leave, in order: a failed eSewa or Khalti payment with a real customer, month-end VAT work without a
sales register, manual `.com` orders, and re-entering unpaid invoices and domains.
