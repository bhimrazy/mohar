Your company name is the brand clients see: in the sidebar, on sign-in pages, in browser titles, emails and invoices.

## Company details

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Settings → Company**.

2. Fill in:
    - **Company name**: shown everywhere as your brand;
    - **Billing email address**: printed on invoices and used as the reply-to address for client email;
    - **Default country**: pre-selected for new clients;
    - **Date format**;
    - **Daily automation time**: when the daily billing run starts, in server time. The default is 03:00.

3. Select **Save company settings**.

    <Screenshot name="settings-company" alt="Settings, Company section, with company name, billing email, default country, date format and daily automation time." />

</Steps>

## Logo, theme and tagline

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Appearance**.

2. Under **Defaults**, choose the theme for the admin panel, the client area and the storefront.

3. Under **Brand**, add a tagline if you want one. It appears under the logo on sign-in pages and in email footers.
   Select **Save brand**.

    <Screenshot name="appearance" alt="Appearance page with theme defaults on the left and the brand panel on the right." />

</Steps>

The seller details printed on invoices (legal name, address, tax numbers) are under **Setup → Settings → Billing**;
