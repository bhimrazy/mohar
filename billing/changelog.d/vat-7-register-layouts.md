### Added

- **VAT register exports: IRD sales book and PDF (VAT PR 7).** Billing → VAT register has an Export menu with three choices. CSV (detail) is every column, as before. CSV (IRD sales book) follows the sales book prescribed under VAT Rule 23(1)(ज): the seller PAN, name, fiscal year and period as leading rows, then the template's 15 columns in its order with its Nepali headings (export declaration number and date in separate columns, blank for services), then our own columns: AD date, document type, reference invoice and its BS date, currency, amount and rate. PDF is A4 landscape with the header block, page and grand totals and the "printed by" line: date, number, type, buyer, PAN and the amounts on the sales register; number, date, type, invoice, buyer, PAN and the amounts on the note register (which has CSV and PDF, no IRD layout). Every export still needs the export permission and a recent password, and writes one `report.exported` entry with its layout and format. Without the permission, the Export button is disabled and says why. An export refused for size shows its reason on the page.
- Upgrade notes: the seeded Billing role now includes `reports.vat_register.export` and `reports.tax_note_register.export`, which `reports.*` does not grant because they are sensitive. `php artisan app:install` resets the system roles, so an existing Billing role gains both, with an audit entry. No schema change.
