# WHMCS parity study, slice C: recent evolution and pain points

Status: complete (2026-10-18). Author: product manager agent. Not yet reviewed by the CTO.

## 1. Scope, method and sources

Scope: (a) what WHMCS added or changed in the 8.x and 9.x lines, because current WHMCS users will expect these;
(b) what hosts complain about and ask for in public; (c) what comparable products claim to do better. Each item is
checked against Mohar on `develop` at commit `b7c30d8`. Slice A (`admin-and-client.md`) covers the admin and client
surface in detail. Slice B (`developer-architecture.md`) covers the API, hooks and module contracts. This slice
covers neither of those in depth.

Method: public pages read with WebFetch on **2026-10-18**. Every row gives a link. All wording is ours.
Community claims are opinions. They show what people complain about and do not prove anything about the product.

Sources read (all on 2026-10-18):

- WHMCS release notes: the index at https://docs.whmcs.com/releases/ and the release-notes page for each version
  from 8.0 to 9.1, e.g. https://docs.whmcs.com/releases/9-1/9-1-release-notes/ (the URL pattern is
  `/releases/<m-n>/<m-n>-release-notes/`). Also the REST API introduction at
  https://developers.whmcs.com/rest-api/introduction and the 9.0.6 changelog mirror at
  https://installatron.com/updatefeed/whmcs_9_0_6.
- WHMCS feature-request board: https://requests.whmcs.com/ (the top-voted list on the front page) and
  https://requests.whmcs.com/idea/billing-term-change.
- WHMCS community: threads 326864, 305879, 356511, 248385, 268937, 261917, 324298, 290765 and 225721 (linked
  where they are used).
- Reviews: Trustpilot https://www.trustpilot.com/review/whmcs.com (3.5/5, 945 reviews, read 2026-10-18).
  Capterra and GetApp returned HTTP 403. Rows that rely on them quote only search-result snippets and say so.
- Trade press and forums: webhosting.today (the 2026 price piece), LowEndSpirit (thread 2763), the 20i blog
  (a competitor, so biased), the Katamaze "Leap" README on GitHub, and The Register and PCWorld (past incidents).
- Security: the CVE-2026-29204 summary at cve.halosecurity.com.
- Competitors: blesta.com/features and /pricing, fossbilling.org, hostbillapp.com/features, upmind.com and
  paymenter.org.
- Regulation: India GST e-invoicing (taxguru.in, invoicera.com) and Nepal CBMS (vatupdate.com, ratopati.com).

Not reachable: Reddit is blocked to our fetcher, and Web Hosting Talk thread pages return 403. One WHT thread
title is cited where noted, and no WHT post content is used. A browser-based pass could add Reddit and WHT evidence
later.

Mohar status values: **Built** (in code on `develop`, path given), **Partial** (part of it is built),
**Specified** (in a spec, no code), **In progress** (in a branch or worktree), **Missing** (no spec and no code, or
explicitly out of scope for v1), **N/A** (we deliberately will not do it). Paths are relative to `billing/`.

Priority: **P0** = needed to win switchers, **P1** = expected soon after launch, **P2** = later or niche.

## 5. Top gaps and opportunities

1. **Payment gateways: Stripe, PayPal and Razorpay (UPI and RBI-compliant cards)**. Owner: Billing. P0. Write
   specs and build the three drivers on the existing `GatewayDriver` and `HostedPaymentDriver` contracts. The
   Nepal drivers alone do not cover switchers who sell abroad or in India.
2. **Control panel provisioning drivers** (cPanel/WHM, Plesk, DirectAdmin, then Proxmox or Virtualizor). Owner:
   Services. P0. Only the `none` and `log` drivers exist, and no switcher can move without their panel.
3. **Registrar drivers** (ResellerClub, Namecheap, .np manual). Owner: Domains. P0. Build `registrars.md`
   starting with the prerequisite PR (issue #12).
4. **WHMCS importer, plus a CSV import for clients**. Owner: Migration. P0. Build `migration.md` and add a CSV
   stage for hosts coming from spreadsheets or other systems.
5. **Merge Notifications, then add WhatsApp and SMS channels**. Owner: Notifications. P0 for email, P1 for the
   other channels. WhatsApp alerts are WHMCS's second-highest request (560 votes) and the norm in South Asia.
6. **Automation watchdog and a System Health page**. Owner: Platform. P1. Alert staff when the daily run is
   missed or late, or a worker is down. Check the queue, scheduler, storage, mail and proxy settings. This answers
   the "silent cron" complaint directly.
7. **Safe upgrades**. Owner: Platform and DevOps. P1. A versioned contract policy, an extension compatibility
   manifest, and an `upgrade` command with backup, migration preflight, rollback and history.
8. **Public REST API with scoped tokens and OpenAPI, including headless checkout and invoice payment**. Owner:
   Core, with Orders and Billing. P1. WHMCS only reached this in 9.0 and 9.1, and Upmind and Paymenter lead
   with it.
9. **Numbered credit and debit note documents**: PDF, admin list and client-area list. Owner: Billing. P1.
   Promote the existing SHOULD to a MUST; WHMCS 9.x made this the norm.
10. **E-invoicing and tax checks**: India IRN, Nepal CBMS, live VIES. Owner: Billing. P1. Add a pluggable
    "tax authority reporter" contract. Larger hosts in our two home markets need it, and WHMCS's offer is
    Belgium-only.
11. **Captcha drivers**: Turnstile, hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA v3. Owner: Platform, used by Orders and Support. P1.
    Put one contract behind signup, checkout, guest tickets and password reset. Turnstile is a top-5 WHMCS
    request.
12. **Support parity**: scheduled ticket actions, and IMAP mailbox import over OAuth2 (Google, Microsoft).
    Owner: Support. P1. Small hosts use Workspace or 365 mailboxes rather than an inbound-mail provider.
13. **Renewal flexibility**: on-demand early renewals and cycles of 4, 5 and 10 years. Owner: Services and
    Catalog. P1. Both are long-standing WHMCS asks (306 votes for longer cycles).
14. **Catalog and promotion requests from the WHMCS board**: dependent configurable options, promotions that
    exclude configurable options, and more than one client group. Owner: Catalog, Orders and Platform. P1.
    These are cheap wins against requests open for years.
15. **Say what we already do better**. Owner: Product. Opportunity. Client billing-term change (WHMCS's top
    request, 807 votes, about 13 years old) is built. So are immutable invoices with a ledger, scheduler-only
    automation and readable source. Put these in the switcher landing page and the importer report.

Open owner decisions this slice raises: the licence and price model (compare Blesta's owned licence and unlimited
clients), the support model, and a published security policy.
