Tax is worked out when an invoice is created and stored on it. Changing a rule later only affects new invoices.

## Settings

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Settings → Billing**.

2. Enter your seller details as they must appear on invoices: legal name, address, billing email and phone, and your
   tax numbers (GSTIN and PAN in India, the VAT/PAN number in Nepal).

3. Turn on **Enable tax**, then choose:
    - **Prices are**: inclusive or exclusive of tax;
    - **Tax calculation**: per invoice line or per invoice;
    - **Seller tax country** and **Seller state**, which decide whether Indian GST is intra-state or inter-state;
    - **Taxed item kinds**, for example services, domains and setup fees.

</Steps>

## Tax rules

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Tax rules** and select **Add tax rule**.

2. Give the rule a **Name** (printed on invoices), a **Rate (%)**, a **Level**, and the **Country** and **State** it
   applies to. For India, **Supply** chooses intra-state or inter-state. Use **Effective from** for a rate that starts
   on a future date.

3. Save. The list shows every rule and where it applies.

    <Screenshot name="tax-rules" alt="Tax rules page with the tax settings summary and rules for CGST, SGST, IGST, Nepal VAT and New York sales tax." />

</Steps>

<Aside title="Examples">
	- **Nepal VAT:** one rule, level 1, country NP, 13%.
	- **India GST:** CGST 9% (level 1) and SGST 9% (level 2) for intra-state supply, and IGST 18% (level 1) for
	  inter-state supply, all with country IN.
	Check current rates with your accountant before you go live.
</Aside>
