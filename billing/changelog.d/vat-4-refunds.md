### Changed

- **Refunds of a numbered tax invoice become credit notes only when the provider has paid (Nepal VAT mode).** While the mode is switched on, a staff refund of a payment on a paid, numbered invoice reserves its credit note first (both caps are checked, no number is taken), is sent to the provider, and becomes exactly that note when the provider confirms: from the provider's callback, Check refund or staff resolution. A decline releases the reservation and the amount; an unknown answer leaves it pending with Check refund only, and a late answer after the refund is settled changes nothing. A refund the provider pays before the mode's start day is booked the old way, dated before it. Staff give a reason when they refund such a payment: the refund dialog has a Reason field, and the reason is printed on the credit note.  A payment that placed account credit (an overpayment) gets that credit taken back first, and only the rest becomes a note. A refund the provider reports on its own becomes an automatic note (`refunded_at_provider`) once per provider refund id; what the invoice's cap cannot take as a note is taken back from the credit that notes placed, and anything left goes to Disputes to review. The credit taken back for such a payment is held when the refund is reserved and returned if the provider does not pay, so no second refund can take it again. A refund recorded as made outside is one transaction. Refunding a payment kept on a numbered invoice that was cancelled in the mode is a further credit note. A declined answer after the provider confirmed a refund puts it on Disputes to review. The mode cannot be switched off while a refund is waiting for the provider. The VAT register keeps every stretch the mode was on: documents numbered under it stay listed after a switch-off and a re-enable, the page says since when the mode is off, and documents numbered while it was off are not counted as unclassified.
- Upgrade notes: one migration adds a nullable `tax_note_id` (foreign key, restrict, with a partial index) to `transactions`, expand only; nothing is backfilled.
