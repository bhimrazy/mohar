Most invoices are created for you: one when an order is placed, and renewal invoices before each service's due date
(see [Automation](../automation/)). Online payments mark them paid on their own. You step in for offline payments,
refunds and exceptions.

<Screenshot name="invoices" alt="Invoices list with status, client, totals and due dates." />

## Record an offline payment

<Steps>

1. Go to **Billing → Invoices** and open the invoice.

2. Select **Record payment**. Enter the amount, the date, the gateway (for example Bank transfer) and, in
   **Transaction ID**, the reference from your bank.

3. Save. A part payment lowers the balance and leaves the invoice unpaid; when the balance reaches zero the invoice is
   marked paid and any service waiting for payment is set up.

    <Screenshot name="invoice" alt="An unpaid invoice with totals, line items, the bill-to details and the Record payment button." />

</Steps>

## Other actions on an invoice

The **⋯** menu and the transaction list on the invoice page hold the less common actions:

- **Apply credit**: use the client's credit balance towards the invoice.
- **Mark paid (write off)**: close the invoice without money. It is recorded as a write-off, not as income.
- **Refund payment** on a transaction: refund in full or in part through the gateway when it supports refunds, or
  **Record only** when you refunded outside the app.
- **Send reminder** and **Download PDF**.

Numbers, tax and the client's address are fixed when an invoice is issued. Later changes to tax rules or the client's
profile do not alter it.
