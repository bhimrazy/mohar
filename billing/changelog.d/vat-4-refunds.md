### Changed

- **Refunds of a numbered tax invoice become credit notes only when the provider has paid (Nepal VAT mode).** While the mode is switched on, a staff refund of a payment on a paid, numbered invoice reserves its credit note first (both caps are checked, no number is taken), is sent to the provider, and becomes exactly that note when the provider confirms: from the provider's callback, Check refund or staff resolution. A decline releases the reservation and the amount; an unknown answer leaves it pending with Check refund only, and a late answer after the refund is settled changes nothing. A refund the provider pays before the mode's start day is booked the old way, dated before it. Staff give a reason when they refund such a payment: the refund dialog has a Reason field, and the reason is printed on the credit note. A refund to account credit is refused on these invoices (issue a credit note settled to account credit). A payment that placed account credit (an overpayment) gets that credit taken back first, and only the rest becomes a note. A refund the provider reports on its own becomes an automatic note (`refunded_at_provider`) once per provider refund id; what the invoice's cap cannot take as a note is taken back from the credit that notes placed, and anything left goes to Disputes to review. The VAT register keeps the documents numbered under the mode after it is switched off. Refunding account credit through the gateway and showing the mode in the menu follow in later steps.
- Upgrade notes: one migration adds a nullable `tax_note_id` (foreign key, restrict, with a partial index) to `transactions`, expand only; nothing is backfilled.
