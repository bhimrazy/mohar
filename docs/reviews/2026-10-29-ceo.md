## 3. Risks to the business

- **Tax rules unanswered, now live behind a switch.** VAT mode is on in the demo and a host can turn it on (#464).
  CBMS applicability, note numbering per fiscal year (D2), FX rate and zero-rated exports (D6), A1 to A11, and the
  three #464 register questions have waited for an accountant since 10-19 (10 days). A wrong assumption is now a
  wrong tax return, not a wrong spec. No payment stores its exchange rate to the default currency (#45), which D6
  will need.
- **All external proof is self-made, and there is more of it.** eSewa, Khalti, connectIPS, Cybersource, Razorpay,
  Stripe, ResellerClub, Namecheap, WHM and the WHMCS API: each is checked only against our own simulator. A misread
  public doc sits in both the driver and the simulator. Known unknown: a real NCHL `.pfx` may use legacy encryption
  that OpenSSL 3 refuses (state.md).
- **No release.** `main` is a landing page; no tag, no image, no tarball a host can install. Waiting on #406 and on
  the owner's approval of the first release run.
- **Mail.** Transport only through `.env`; when mail is down, adding a client login or resending an invite returns a
  500 (#443); a note email can say "PDF attached" when the render failed (#506).
- **Money edge cases open.** #507 (a Stripe "succeeded" refund racing a failed report can book an unmarked row); the
  #464 re-review gap (no test for the amount-differs branch). Stripe is not the Nepal path, but it is money.
- **Data.** KYC citizenship scans decrypt only with the same `APP_KEY`; losing it loses them. Billing-role staff can
  export buyer PANs (#480; release note owed). No privacy notice or data-processing terms for hosts exist yet; not
  reviewed against Nepal's Privacy Act.
- **Owner-gated.** Accountant, release approval, wallet UAT, bcrypt cost (#433), five #211 behaviours, Nepali IRD CSV
  header labels, whether an emailed note PDF counts as a print, connectIPS enrolment, licence and NPR price.

## 4. A Nepali host's first week (400 clients, WHMCS, cPanel reseller, .com.np and .com, VAT registered)

- **Day 1, install.** Docker install with HTTPS and Nepal defaults on 4 GB, restore steps tested. No released
  version to pin: they install from a branch. Mail needs `.env` edits on the server; no test send in admin.
- **Day 2, company and money.** PAN, VAT 13%, lakh grouping, BS beside AD. They switch Nepal VAT mode on. The
  accountant gets the sales book in IRD layout by BS month, CSV or PDF, and can see how a paid invoice is corrected
  with a credit note. The accountant's first question, "is this CBMS-compliant?", has no answer in the product.
- **Day 3, clients and services.** CSV client import with review and undo; cPanel accounts import from WHM with
  values accepted and priced cycles. Unpaid invoices are re-typed. Domains are re-entered; ResellerClub domains of
  clients imported per-client cannot be linked (#372).
- **Day 4, payments.** eSewa, Khalti, connectIPS and NIC Asia cards all appear in settings. The host enters live
  merchant keys, and the first real payment is a customer's. A portal refund is caught by the next day's re-lookup.
- **Day 5, domains and renewals.** `.np` guidance in one place; `.com` renewals through ResellerClub once linked.
  Renewal invoices go out with the PDF attached.
- **Week end.** Client area has the store, cart and invoices with PAN blocks; notes visible to clients. No aging or
  income report for the owner's month-end; English only.

What makes them leave, in order: a failed or stuck live wallet payment, re-typing unpaid invoices and domains,
email not arriving with no way to fix it in admin, and an accountant who will not sign off the VAT output.
