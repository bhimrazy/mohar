### Changed

- **Refunds of a numbered tax invoice become credit notes only when the provider has paid (Nepal VAT mode).** While the mode is switched on, a staff refund of a payment on a paid, numbered invoice reserves its credit note first (both caps are checked, no number is taken), is sent to the provider, and becomes exactly that note when the provider confirms: from the provider's callback, Check refund or staff resolution. A decline releases the reservation and the amount; an unknown answer leaves it pending with Check refund only, and a late answer after the refund is settled changes nothing. A refund the provider pays before the mode's start day is booked the old way, dated before it. Staff give a reason when they refund such a payment: the refund dialog has a Reason field, and the reason is printed on the credit note. A refund to account credit is refused on these invoices (issue a credit note settled to account credit), and so is a refund of a payment that also placed account credit until that part is taken back. The credit-first split, refunds the provider reports without a reservation, refunding account credit through the gateway and showing the mode in the menu follow in later steps.
- Upgrade notes: one migration adds a nullable `tax_note_id` (foreign key, restrict, with a partial index) to `transactions`, expand only; nothing is backfilled.
