Products are what clients order: hosting plans, VPS, SSL certificates. Each product belongs to a group, and groups
are how the store is organised.

## Create a group

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Product groups** and select **New group**.

2. Give it a name clients will see, such as "Shared Hosting", and save.

    <Screenshot name="product-groups" alt="Product groups list." />

</Steps>

## Create a product

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Products** and select **New product**.

2. Choose the group and the type, enter the name, and select **Create product**. The product's page opens with its
   settings in tabs.

3. On the **Details** tab, under **Billing**, keep **Recurring** for plans billed every month or year and leave
   **Taxable** on if tax applies. Under **Automation**, choose when the account is set up, for example **When the
   first payment is received**. Save.

4. Open the **Pricing** tab. Turn on each billing cycle you sell, in each currency, and enter the price. Clients only
   see the cycles priced in their own currency. Select **Save prices**.

    <Screenshot name="product-pricing" alt="The Pricing tab of a product, with monthly, annual and biennial prices in US dollars, Indian rupees and Nepalese rupees." />

5. Open the **Provisioning** tab to connect the product to a server. See
   [Connect a cPanel/WHM server](../cpanel-server/#use-the-server-for-a-product).

</Steps>

Prices in other currencies can be converted from the default currency with each currency's rounding rule. Lock a
price to keep the amount you typed. Changing a price never changes what existing clients pay.
