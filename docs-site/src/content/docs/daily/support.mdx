Clients open tickets from the client area, guests from the public support page, and anyone by email once email
piping is set up.

## Set up departments

<Steps>

1. Go to **Support → Departments** and add a department for each team, such as Sales, Technical Support and Billing.

2. Give each one its own email address. Replies to clients are sent from it, and email to it opens a ticket in that
   department.

3. Choose which staff belong to each department. Staff see only their departments' tickets unless their role allows
   all tickets.

</Steps>

## Answer tickets

Go to **Support → Tickets**. The tabs filter by **Open**, **Awaiting reply**, **My tickets**, **Unassigned**,
**Flagged** and **Closed**.

<Screenshot name="tickets" alt="Tickets list with tabs, filters, and tickets showing requester, status, priority, assignee and last reply." />

A staff reply sets the ticket to **Answered**; a client reply sets it to **Customer reply**. You can add private notes,
assign the ticket, and use **Support → Canned replies** for answers you send often. Tickets left waiting on the
client are closed automatically after the hours set in **Auto-close idle tickets after** under
**Setup → Settings → Support**.

## Receive tickets by email

Email piping turns messages sent to a department address into tickets and replies. A mail relay posts each message
to the app as JSON, signed with a secret you share with it.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Settings → Inbound email webhook** and set a secret of at least 32 characters. It is write-only:
   to change it, enter a new one.

2. Set up your mail relay to post each message for the department addresses to
   `https://<your domain>/api/v1/inbound/email/generic`, with an HMAC-SHA256 signature of the request body, made
   with the same secret, in the `X-Support-Signature` header.

3. Send a test email to a department address, then check **Support → Email piping**. Every received message is listed
   with what happened to it: a new ticket, a reply, or the reason it was held back.

</Steps>

Replies from addresses the app cannot match to the ticket's client are held for staff to review, not added to the
ticket.
