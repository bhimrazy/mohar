### Fixed

- **The Awaiting reply queue no longer sorts by two subqueries per ticket (#51).** Each ticket now stores the time of its oldest unanswered customer reply (`first_unanswered_at`), and the queue reads it from an index that matches its order (pinned first, oldest unanswered, answered last). Repeated customer replies still do not move a ticket back. The index covers tickets that are not closed and not merged. The upgrade migration adds the column and the index and fills the column in batches of 1,000 tickets (about 2 seconds for 100,000 tickets on PostgreSQL); it blocks no writes. Upgrade note: the previous release does not maintain the column, so a reply recorded by it while both releases run can leave a wrong value (a waiting customer sorts last, or an answered ticket sorts early). After the old release has stopped, run `php artisan support:recompute-unanswered --since="<deploy start time>"` once.

### Security

- **Merging tickets replaces a guest target ticket's access key (#18).** A guest ticket opened under someone else's email address can no longer keep its old link into a merged thread, even when both tickets used the same address. The previous link stops working, and a new link is emailed to the target ticket's requester address (new template `ticket.link_replaced`, subject "New link to your ticket", always sent). A client ticket's key is replaced only when the merged tickets have different requesters.

### Changed

- **Staff ticket mail goes to the people the ticket concerns (#30).** A new ticket mails the department's members who have new-ticket mail on; a customer reply mails the assignee, or the members who have reply mail on while the ticket is unassigned; a ticket moved to another department mails the new department's members only when it ends up unassigned (a ticket that keeps its assignee sends no mail). Recipients need `support.tickets.view` and access to the ticket's department (member, or `support.tickets.access_all`), so an assignee removed from the department or a subscriber limited to other departments is no longer mailed. When nobody qualifies (a department without members, an assignee who is inactive), the mail goes to the support alert subscribers who can open the department. Upgrade note: staff who are not department members no longer receive new-ticket or reply mail for that department, whatever their role's email subscriptions say.
