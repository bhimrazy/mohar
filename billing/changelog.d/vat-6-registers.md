### Added

- **VAT register (Billing → VAT register, #428).** Two tabs: the VAT sales register (every tax invoice, credit note and debit note dated in the period) and the credit and debit note register (notes only, with reason, settlement and who issued them). Choose a BS month (default: the last closed month on the company calendar), a BS fiscal year (1 Shrawan to the end of Asar, with one totals row per month) or an AD date range. Amounts come from the snapshot stored when each document was numbered and from the notes, converted to the reporting currency (NPR by default) with each document's stored rate, half up per document; credit notes are negative. Each page shows BS and AD dates, totals per document kind and the net, the "Return figures" (net taxable sales, net VAT, exempt and export sales), and warnings for business buyers without a PAN and for documents without a stored rate. Export as CSV (UTF-8 with BOM) through the report pipeline. The `detail` and `ird` layouts and the PDF follow in VAT PR 7.
- Upgrade notes: new permissions `reports.vat_register.view`, `reports.vat_register.export`, `reports.tax_note_register.view` and `reports.tax_note_register.export`, registered by the report registry. The export permissions are sensitive: a wildcard never grants them, and every export asks for a recent password. The seeded Billing role gets both view permissions through `reports.*`; grant the export permissions explicitly. The screen and the export are limited to 60 requests a minute per staff member. One migration adds two partial indexes on `invoices` (`bs_period` and `issue_date`, where `numbered_at` is set), built concurrently on PostgreSQL.
