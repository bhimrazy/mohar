Bank transfer needs no provider account. Clients see your instructions on unpaid invoices, pay from their bank, and
you record the payment when it arrives.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Setup → Gateways → Bank transfer**.

2. In **Payment instructions**, write your bank name, account name and number, and anything else the client needs.
   Write `{invoice}` where the invoice number should appear, so clients put it in the payment reference.

    <Screenshot name="gateway-bank" alt="Bank transfer gateway settings with the payment instructions field." />

3. Turn on **Active** and **Offer to clients**, then select **Save gateway**.

</Steps>

## Record a payment

When the money is in your account, open the invoice under **Billing → Invoices**, select **Record payment**, and
enter the amount, the date and, in **Transaction ID**, your bank's reference. The invoice is marked paid when its balance reaches zero. See
[Invoices and payments](../../daily/invoices/).
