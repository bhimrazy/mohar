### Added

- **Branded error pages.** A signed-in customer or staff member who reaches a missing, forbidden, expired (419), rate-limited or failed page now sees the host's brand with a plain explanation and two ways out, instead of the framework's bare "404 | Not Found".

### Changed

- **Country is a list of names in the client account, the admin client form and the domain contacts form**, not a free two-letter code (the stored value is still the ISO code).

### Fixed

- **A currency symbol no longer wraps away from its amount in the phone-width dashboard cards.**
- **Empty lists no longer say "No clients" a second time under their empty state.**
- **The invoice transactions table keeps its Refund button in view at 1440px**, and TLD badges are capitalised the same way as on the storefront.
