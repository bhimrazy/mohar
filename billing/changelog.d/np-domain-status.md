### Fixed

- **A domain the client registered at the registry (such as a .np name) no longer reads "Active" with "After registration" (#420).** On the client and admin domain lists and detail pages it shows "Self-registered"; expiry reads "At the registry" (or the date, if staff recorded one); auto-renew is replaced by "At the registry". The auto-renew switch, the renewal price, the next-due fields and the renewal actions are hidden. Turning auto-renew on, creating a renewal invoice (client or staff) and the staff "renew" registrar command are refused with an error. Domain email merge fields gain `domain.status_label`, `domain.registered_by_client` and `domain.expiry_label`.
- **The daily renewal run no longer invoices names registered at the registry.** Upgrade note: a migration turns auto-renew off for existing domains under such extensions, and new ones start with it off. Rolling the migration back turns it on again for those domains.
