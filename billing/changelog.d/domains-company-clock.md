### Fixed

- **Domains follows the company calendar (ADR 0013 Amendment 1).** Domains no longer reads the UTC date. For a Kathmandu company, between 00:00 and 05:45 local time: an ordered domain's due and invoice dates, and a registered or transferred-in domain's registration date and the expiry the module works out itself, use the company date; the "expiring in 30 days" dashboard counts, the expiring filter and the client "domains to renew" count drop a domain once its expiry day is over on the company calendar; "days until expiry" (domain pages and email merge fields) counts from the company date; a TLD's renewal window opens on the company day; the registrar log prune keeps entries from the start of the company day. Renewal invoicing, expiry reminders and the grace and redemption steps already ran on the company date (the automation run date) and are covered by new boundary tests. Upgrade notes: no schema or permission change; dates already stored are not rewritten. The offline log registrar no longer invents an expiry from the UTC date: on a registration, a transfer or a sync without a known expiry it returns none and the module dates it from the company calendar. ResellerClub's contact change now returns `transfer_lock_days: 60` and the module dates the transfer lock (`transfer_lock_until`) from the company date, so no driver reads settings. Known gap: ResellerClub's own expiry arithmetic compares registrar dates with each other and was left as is. The arch scan baseline no longer lists Domains; the UTC-date scan now covers `app/Modules/Domains`, with no file exceptions there.
