## 3. Quality

Recurring defect classes in the 23 PRs read (blocking items):

1. **Gates claimed, not run.** PHPStan red at review on #482 (B1) and #432 (B1); CI red at review on #491 (B4) and
   #428. Of 75 CI failures, 7 were Pint, 6 PHPStan, 4 `vp check`, 3 fragments: all catchable locally in a minute.
   `module-engineer` runs on Sonnet; the formatting and static gates are the ones it skips.
2. **Money branches with no test.** #505 B1 (delete `flagFailedAtProvider` and all 158 targeted tests pass); #464
   re-review (amount-differs branch untested). Mutation-style probes found these; nothing else would.
3. **A claimed state the code never creates.** #464 B2 (200 "staff review" with nothing on the list), #491 B1 (books a
   payment whose credit leg failed), #447 B2 (warning and changelog promise a reclassification that never runs).
4. **Units and amounts.** #491 B2 (accepts an amount 100 times smaller), #464 B1 (credit refunded twice), #478 B1
   (Renew now amount ignores grace fee, tax, currency).
5. **Locks and races.** #464 B3 (lock-order deadlock), #505/#507 (succeeded-vs-failed race, left open by decision).
6. **Spec MUSTs missed.** #491 B3, #482 B2. The PR body does not map criteria to tests.
7. **Green alone, red together.** `develop` broke three times from two PRs each green on its own: #425 (clock scan vs
   Namecheap), #466 (#459 scan vs #461 route), #472 (#462 signature vs #464 call). Whole-repo scans and kernel
   signature changes are where this happens.

#495, #500 on #489; #498 and #499 on #497; #506 (note email says "attached" when render fails). None reached a user;

## 4. Process

Gates that earn their cost:

- Combined review plus re-reviewer on money: every real money defect above came from them, most with a probe that
  failed on the old code. The architect on kernel PRs found the shape problems (#432 B2 to B4, #482 B1, #489, #500).
- The issue plan (#455): 104 open issues on 10-23, 56 now (73 closed, 25 opened). The waves were done in two days
  instead of five, and the inflow rule (follow-ups into the open PR or a package) cut new issues.
- Sharded CI, fragments, deny rules: each removed a measured cost.

Gates that do not, or are not real:

- **The re-reviewer on the last round of money PRs is skipped.** The rule says a money PR is not marked ready while
  the latest verdict is "Changes requested". #491 (connectIPS money driver) merged 16 min after a "Changes requested"
  re-review, with round 3 "read from the diff"; #505 merged after fix round 2 without a re-review; #428 after two
  "Changes requested" re-reviews. The CTO's reads were careful, but the written gate and the practice differ on
  exactly the PRs the gate exists for.
- **The two-round cap.** #491, #428 and #478 had third rounds; none went to the architect for a redesign. In practice
  the cap is "three rounds, the third read by the CTO". Either write that or enforce the stop.
- **The 12-hour review round.** Rounds ran on 10-23 (CTO, architecture), 10-24 (CEO) and 10-29 (CEO, CTO). The last
  whole-`develop` security round is 10-20, before the gateway foundation, three payment drivers, the log backstop and
  step-up changes merged.

CTO calls that cost effort:

- Launching 13 PRs at 19:00 before the session ended, and up to 22 open at once on 10-23, against the 8-open rule.
  Again today: 6 PRs (#510 to #515) opened between 05:29 and 05:36.
- Merging money PRs with known items and turning them into follow-up PRs, whose reviews then spawn the next
  follow-up (gateway four-PR chain). One more fix round on #489 would likely have been cheaper than three PRs.
- Leaving ADR 0010's snapshot to wave 5 while 33 kernel-changelog fragments landed without it (§5).
- Hygiene: 126 worktrees and 57 GB with no open PR older than today; disk at 83% against the 85% limit;
  `storage-cleanup.sh` "every 30 minutes" is not happening.

Concurrency: not measured (no resource-monitor log read). The load was 4.1 after a reboot. The 10-23 overload was not

## 5. Technical debt and risks for the next month

- **Kernel drift from its own ADRs.** ADR 0011 says every kernel event implements `ShouldDispatchAfterCommit` and the
  four older mechanisms are removed. 5 of 118 event classes do; 44 call sites still use `AfterCommit::dispatch`,
  `DB::afterCommit` or `ShouldHandleEventsAfterCommit`. The arch test checks `DomainEvent`, not the interface. A new
  event fired inside a transaction that rolls back is still delivered unless its call site remembered the helper.
- **No API snapshot (ADR 0010, Accepted 10-18).** `Kernel::API_VERSION` and `kernel:api-snapshot` do not exist; 33
  kernel-changelog fragments are unassembled; kernel signatures changed in at least #462, #482, #489, #500, #505.
  P12a (#513, draft) is building it now. Until it merges, a signature change is caught only by phpstan on whichever
  PR happens to call it (#472).
- **A backlog outside the tracker.** `state.md` "Deferred, not yet in the issue plan" holds about 30 items from #462,
  #464, #468, #471, #474, #478, #480 to #482 and #503, including money ones (#464 B2 branch untested; staff note
  overwritten). 56 open issues understate the work.
- **Money paths verified only on simulators.** Six gateway drivers and the refund state machine; #507's race is open
  by decision. The first live recording will find what simulators cannot (amount units, as #491 B2 shows).
- **Redaction size.** Redactor 1,331 lines, 112 probe files; every new driver adds call sites.
- **Machine.** Disk at 83%; every new worktree is about 1 GB, so the limit is about 10 worktrees away.
- **Big PRs.** 8 PRs over 2,000 changed lines gross (#428 2,756; #376 2,733; #369 2,576; #452 2,488; #478 2,419;

## 6. Changes

1. **Make the kernel ADRs mechanical, in P12a or right after it.** Arch test: every `App\Events` class implements
   `ShouldDispatchAfterCommit` (with a frozen baseline of today's 113 that shrinks); CI step: `kernel:api-snapshot`
   diff fails without a kernel fragment. Effect: no more #472-style breaks; ADR 0011 converges instead of drifting.
2. **Stop merging money PRs over a "Changes requested" re-review.** One re-review per fix round on money, auth and
   data deletion, as written; if a third round is needed, the architect decides redesign or merge. Rewrite the cap as
   practised for everything else. Effect: the strict loop means what it says; follow-up chains like #489 to #500
   collapse into the original PR.
3. **Engineers run the cheap gates in a pre-push hook, not by memory.** Pint, phpstan on changed files, `vp check`,
   the fragment check, and `tests/Arch` plus `tests/Feature/Security` (the whole-repo scans). Effect: about 20 of 75
   CI failures and two of three `develop` breaks caught before review.
4. **Pace to the session, not the slot count.** No new build in the last 90 min of a session; at most 8 open PRs; a
   whole-`develop` security round now (three drivers, foundation and backstop since 10-20). Record every gap over
   30 min in `history.md`. Effect: the 500 to 700 min tail goes; the 3.6-day gap becomes a known cost.
5. **Move the `state.md` deferred list into package issues and fix the docs.** One issue per package with checklist
   items; align lifecycle steps 3, 7, 8, the brief template and the review cadence with practice; run
   `storage-cleanup.sh --apply`. Effect: one backlog number that is true, and rules agents can follow as written.
