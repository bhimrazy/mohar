## 2. Throughput: where the wall-clock time went

queue (#362, #364, #369, #373, #376) have been open 0.7 to 6.2 h.

Top 5 bottlenecks, ranked by wall-clock time lost:

1. **The machine stopped: 3 h 16 min of zero output.** The last commit or review was at 07:24. A shutdown stalled at
   07:25 (`shutdown_stall_2026-10-23-131011`) and the Mac rebooted at 10:41 (`sysctl kern.boottime`). During the gap
   there was no commit on any branch, no review and no CI run. #358, #360, #361 and #363 were signed off at about

   1-minute load was 17.7 on 10 cores and free memory was under the 2 GB rule. This is the second stall in two days.
2. **The tail after sign-off runs one PR at a time.** The chain is: signed off while still a draft, then marked ready
   (CI skips drafts), then CI, then the changelog conflicts with the previous merge, then a refresh, a second CI run
   and the merge. Six PRs signed off between 11:28 and 12:17 were all marked ready together at 12:19, after waiting 2
   to 51 min (#364: 51, #365: 39, #362: 30). `merge-queue.sh` then merges them in order, with roughly one CI run
   (8.8 min, of which 7.5 min is two Pest suites run one after the other) per refresh. Because drafts get no CI,
   #363's `SensitiveRouteStepUpTest` failure only showed up after its sign-off.
3. **Fix rounds on masking and money.** #345 had 8 review posts and 3 fix rounds (masking gaps, then a test that

   invoice's transaction. #337 had 5 posts over 10 h. Masking is still repaired one call site at a time; ADR 0023
   exists to end that.
4. **Reviews wait behind bursts of launches.** 10 PRs were opened between 06:42 and 07:20. The board did not reach
   #365, #368 or #369 until 11:01 to 11:08 (the stall plus too few free slots). There were 12 PRs open at 12:58,
   against the rule "no new builds while eight or more PRs await review".
5. **Stacks and shared files force sequences.** VAT 1a (#369) blocks 1b (#385; based on `develop`, so its diff

## 4. Process changes

Adopt now (CTO):

1. **Open PRs ready, or with the `ci` label from the first push.** CI then runs alongside the review, so sign-off
   has CI evidence and failures like #363 surface before it. Expected to cut 20 to 50 min of draft wait per PR.
2. **Split CI into two parallel jobs, SQLite and PostgreSQL.** The job takes 8.8 min today and the two Pest suites
   take 2.8 and 4.7 min. Expected: about 5.5 min per run, which speeds every refresh in the queue.
3. **Pace launches with the queue.** Open at most 4 PRs per 30 min, and start no new build while 8 or more PRs are
   open. Admit agents by load, not by headcount (§3).
4. **A fix round fixes findings only.** New behaviour, such as #362's staff resolve, goes in a separate PR. Masking
   fixes wait for ADR 0023 instead of another round per call site.
5. **A single combined review is enough** for non-money follow-ups under 300 lines (#371, #380, #386, #387): the CTO
   verifies from the diff and CI. Keep the architect plus board plus re-reviewer for money, auth and kernel; they
   found today's real defects (#362 #9, #369's blue-green drift of the note totals, #345 N1).
6. **Record every stall over 30 minutes** in `history.md`, with its cause. Apply the "tests that cannot fail" check
   to adopted probes: a variadic `not->toContain($x, $label)` always passes.

Needs the owner: changelog fragments, auto-merge (`allow_auto_merge` is false; the token cannot set branch
protection), and `.claude/settings.json` deny rules (§6).

## 5. Risks and debt

- **The test PostgreSQL's tmpfs (4 GB) is larger than the Docker VM (3.58 GiB).** #375 justified 4 GB with "the Mac
  has 24 GB", but the tmpfs lives in the VM. The next fill exhausts VM memory instead of returning "disk full", which
  takes down `mohar-pg` and Mailpit with it. Either give the VM 6 GB or more, or set the tmpfs to 2.5 GB and keep the
  small WAL.
- **Machine overload.** Two stalls in two days, a load of 17.7, a `php` process writing 34 GB, 57 merged worktrees.
  Nothing enforces the load rule; it depends on the CTO checking.
- **Masking is spread across call sites until ADR 0023 lands.** The Redactor is 995 lines, files with "probe" in
  their names went from 55 (10-22 16:00) to 90, and #383, #356, #386 and #388 are all open or new. Every new driver
  (Namecheap now, connectIPS later) adds call sites.
- **VAT is a seven-PR chain on Billing files**, with VAT 4 and ADR 0023 step 7 both in the gateway path. Without one
  owner and a fixed order, the two will collide in wave 3.
- **104 open issues** (16 opened and 10 closed in this period), none with an owner. #292 (memory tests that cannot
  fail on macOS and in CI) has been open since 10-22.
- **The written rules lag the practice.** Step 7's forced `develop` merge, and the 8-open-PRs rule, which is not
  followed.
