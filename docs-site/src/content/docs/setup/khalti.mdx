title: Khalti
description: Take payments in Nepalese rupees with Khalti.
---

Clients are sent to Khalti's payment page, then come back to the invoice. The app looks the payment up at Khalti
before it marks the invoice paid. Khalti charges in Nepalese rupees (NPR).

You need a Khalti merchant account.

<Steps>

1. In the Khalti merchant dashboard (`admin.khalti.com`), open **Keys** and copy the live secret key. On a staging
   copy (not production) you can use the key from the test dashboard (`test-admin.khalti.com`) with sandbox mode on.

2. Go to **Setup → Gateways → Khalti**, turn **Sandbox mode** off and paste the **Secret key**.

3. If your invoices are in another currency, set **Charge in currency** to Nepalese rupee.

4. Select **Save gateway**, then **Test connection**. No money moves.

5. Turn on **Active** and save.

</Steps>

Full refunds go through Khalti from the transaction on the invoice (**Refund payment**). Partial refunds are off by
default. A payment the client made from a bank account through Khalti cannot be refunded from here: refund it in the
Khalti merchant portal, then use **Refund payment** with **Record only**.
