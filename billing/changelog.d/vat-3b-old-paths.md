### Changed

- **Cancelling, cancelling lines and late fees on a numbered tax invoice issue notes while Nepal VAT mode is on** (the mode itself stays hidden behind its feature flag until VAT PR 4). Staff cancel, auto-cancel and the modules that cancel an invoice (Orders, Services, Domains) issue one `invoice_cancelled` credit note for the open balance, which credits everything the invoice holds: its lines, a discount line as a negative line of the same note, and every late fee (a debit note, or an old-style fee) as a line of its own; with a payment or applied credit on the invoice the note is scaled to the balance. Cancel lines issues one `lines_cancelled` credit note, or the single cancel note when it cancels the last open line; it is not offered on an invoice with a discount line (cancel the invoice instead). A late fee is a debit note. Staff give a reason, which is printed on the note (the cancel and cancel-lines dialogs ask for it once the mode is in effect); a module's note reads "Cancelled by <module>". Reopen is refused for a numbered tax invoice and shown disabled. Proforma invoices, add-funds invoices and any path before the mode's start day behave as before; a note cannot be issued before the start day.
- **A cancel or a late fee Billing cannot write never stops a run and is never silent.** Auto-cancel and late fees go on past an invoice whose note is refused; the invoice goes on a new "Invoices a credit or debit note could not be issued for" item of the dashboard's Needs attention (it links to just those invoices, and an entry drops off by itself once the invoice is cancelled or paid), with the reason in its activity. When a domain's renewal invoice cannot be cancelled at expiry, no grace or redemption invoice is issued beside it. `Invoicer::cancel` throws `InvoiceNotCancellable` when the invoice stays payable, and Orders, Services and Domains keep their own records open (an order is not cancelled, a renewal's links and pointers are left alone, a domain's renewal invoice is not marked cancelled). Bulk cancel says which numbered invoices it skipped and why.
- Every old path now dates all the rows it writes with one instant taken after its invoice lock, and reads the mode under a shared lock (a staff note too), so enabling the mode waits for transactions in flight and none can write an old-style row dated on or after the start day.
- Enabling the mode is also refused while a refund recorded the old way (pending, with no note reservation) is open on a numbered invoice.
- Upgrade notes: `Invoicer::cancel` takes an optional reason (`?string $reason = null`) and may throw `App\Support\Billing\InvoiceNotCancellable`; implementers of the contract must add the parameter, and callers that mark their own record cancelled must catch the exception. No schema change.
